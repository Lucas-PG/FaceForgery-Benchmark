# Recording-level analysis

Use the campaign's `analyze` stage with a frozen configuration. Outputs live in `analysis/`. Positive labels and larger scores mean forgery. The alert rule is strictly `score > cutoff`; tied scores at the cutoff do not flag.

## Units, fitting and outcomes

- A matched observation comprises one recording with one score for every registered route. The primary cohort weights independent lineage clusters equally within each label. Before observing scores, select the recording with the smallest SHA-256 key per cluster and label. Family-specific results repeat that selection within family. `all_recordings.csv` reports unweighted recording averages descriptively; frames, copies, siblings from a common source and training seeds cannot increase the independent-case count.
- The `dev` split is partitioned deterministically by whole clusters into disjoint fitting and model-selection folds. Fit preprocessing of quality features, ridge-logistic quality rescoring, and route offsets only on the fitting fold. Re-estimate final numerical cutoffs on the `cal` genuine representatives. Do not use any external score to select candidates or operating policies.
- `shared` calibrates the genuine maximum over copies. `route` learns a vector of route offsets by a finite coordinate grid maximizing all-copy fake recall under the dev any-copy budget, then calibrates a fresh common shift. `quality` uses the source score, the registered quality proxy and their interaction on one received video. `native` uses a source native cutoff on every copy. `marginal` fits each source channel separately. For unseen route names, route-specific policies use the most conservative learned offset or cutoff. The registered `source_alpha` is a guard band; a source empirical quantile never establishes an external error guarantee.
- `metrics.csv` contains primary all-copy fake sensitivity and any-copy genuine false alerts plus per-route rates and per-family cells. Intervals there are pointwise, conditional on the selected frozen policy. `patterns.csv` reports matched 0/1/? route patterns. `gaps.csv` measures worst marginal recall minus all-copy recall and any-copy FPR minus worst marginal FPR. Its intervals simultaneously cover the route-specific nested event rates within each row. `discrimination.csv` reports per-route AUROC and standardized-by-width area under the first `alpha` portion of the ROC; random ranking has expected width-normalized partial area `alpha/2`. These are supplementary measures.
- An explicitly failed score counts as a miss for forged records and a potential false alarm for genuine records in enrolled-case performance. See `coverage.csv`, `complete_cases.csv`, and the underlying score failures. A detector without complete scores cannot establish an eligible confirmatory contrast. Permitting omitted input recordings with `data.allow_incomplete` makes the headline output exploratory. Confirm eligibility, class and family coverage, media provenance, and duplicate resolution before interpreting estimates.

## Statistical decisions

The recalibration ceiling is computed for each prespecified frozen baseline. A feasible vector of route cutoffs must meet the false-positive limit separately on every route. The fake fraction exceeding every route's marginal genuine quantile upper-bounds feasible all-copy recall. Genuine lineages in `diagnostic` estimate route quantiles independently of `test` lineages. For rigorous upper confidence, relax each cutoff to a simultaneous *lower* binomial order-statistic bound on its population quantile, then compute an *upper* Clopper–Pearson limit on all-copy test sensitivity. `ceiling.csv` distinguishes the plug-in number from its upper bound. Rank zero is an uninformative lower quantile and yields a vacuous ceiling. This argument covers deterministic monotone recalibration by known route, conditional on stable sampling from the registered target population. A within-route quality-based model can reorder recordings and must be compared directly. If sufficient independent diagnostic examples of both labels are present, `target_calibration.csv` shows an attainable higher-information local policy learned and recalibrated on separate diagnostic folds.

The primary run and comparator IDs, cohorts, fixed seed, margins and multiplicity budget must be declared before test scores are opened. `contrasts.csv` uses paired recording outcomes on identical case IDs. A difference in proportions equals the probability of an improvement minus the probability of a deterioration. Write this difference as d(2w−1), where d is the probability of disagreement and w is the probability the learned model wins given disagreement. Fit exact binomial intervals for d and conditional w (with [0,1] when there are no disagreements). Take the extrema of the product over their simultaneous confidence rectangle. The paired outcomes stay together, and the interval uses their observed dependence. Bonferroni allocation covers the registered cohorts, gates and secondary-backbone checks. No independence between detectors or between routes is required. Exchangeability across independent lineages within a cohort is required. In the special `recalibration_ceiling` row, lower is the learned method's lower bound and upper is the fixed comparator's ceiling; these bound *different* quantities. An inconclusive comparison does not establish equivalence. `calibration_bootstrap.csv` provides a secondary paired sensitivity analysis refitting the primary common cutoff under independent resampling of source calibration genuine units and target fake units; its 95% percentile intervals are descriptive and conditional on development-stage choices.

`decisions.json` records distinct claims:

- `training_claim` applies to the registered primary model and seed, evaluated on every confirmation cohort. It requires the false-alarm cap, gain over augmented training, superiority over fitted quality and route recalibrators and matched controls, a positive pairing effect, clean noninferiority, and a lower sensitivity bound above the frozen baseline ceiling.
- `replicated_training_claim` also requires an explicitly registered second model comparison to pass, plus consistent gain, paired-vs-shuffled direction, clean margin and false-alert cap in the point estimates for every configured matched seed. `replications.csv` and `seed_contrasts.csv` distinguish confirmatory from descriptive comparisons. `seeds.csv` gives run-level variability without pooling predictions.
- `selection_claim` compares two selectors with identical calibrated candidates. One maximizes worst-channel sensitivity subject to channel-wise false alarms; the other maximizes all-copy sensitivity subject to any-copy false alarms. `detector_selection_claim` compares conventional mean-AUROC and paired detector selection using the **same** source-fitted shared policy. Both selection rules use source development only and are adjudicated on external data. Required evidence is a verified recording-level false-alert cap for the paired choice plus the registered detection advantage or a verified false-alarm violation by the conventional choice. `selection.csv` also includes higher-information and native-rule alternatives. Inspect `changed_detector` before attributing a difference to detector selection.

`power.csv` gives finite-sample false-alert power and Monte Carlo paired-gain/clean-margin power under stated planning assumptions (editable under `analysis.power`). These are projections conditional on assumed rates. No test score enters them. Native noninferiority and simultaneous false-alert evidence may require considerably more independent lineages than a marginal 5% test. If the anticipated power is inadequate, revise case acquisition before opening the external outcomes.

**Interpretation and transfer.** The external augmented and clean references must independently establish the same any-copy false-alert cap before their sensitivity contrasts determine the confirmatory decision. A reference with uncertain eligibility still has a reported estimate. `matched_training_claim` tests the registered empirical comparisons, `beyond_recalibration_claim` tests the learned model's error budget and the monotone upper bound, and `training_claim` requires both. The selection output can refute an advantage when the two procedures return the identical operating rule, the proposed selection violates the cap, or both components of the promised advantage can be ruled out. Selection uses the named development candidates at one fixed seed; searching the full table of model/seed/policy results post hoc would require new confirmation.

`route_mixture.csv` gives equal-weight route sensitivity and false alerts. Its uncertainty averages simultaneous route-specific marginal limits on independent recordings. `subgroup_contrasts.csv` estimates paired sensitivity and false-alert changes for each known/new route, their joint endpoint, and registered held-out fake families; false alerts concern the genuine cohort, independent of generator. It includes per-channel and clean-floor ablations under the shared calibration rule. These secondary intervals are pointwise. `cluster_sensitivity.csv` uses all recordings, including siblings, reporting paired differences with descriptive percentile intervals from resampling whole lineage clusters. `power.csv` also sizes the ceiling comparison in a reference scenario with identical route ranks, uniform genuine scores, a power-law fake score CDF calibrated to the assumed ceiling, and nested learned/frozen fake outcomes. This is an explicit planning scenario; deployments can have a much looser bound. Marginal power for one gate never establishes joint power across all gates.

## Enrollment decision before any capture or GPU jobs

Identify actual independent lineage components first. More forged siblings, actors appearing in many paired scenes, repeated routes, and more training seeds cannot substitute for independent cases. Run the `enrollment` stage using the prospective inventory. Archive its counts and explicit assumptions. If suitable source-only pilots exist, freeze sensitivity scenarios from their discordance counts, including a favorable and an adverse scenario. Preserve the originally registered scenario. Decide the populations and attainable claims **before** opening external scores.

The released two-cohort, two-backbone configuration allocates tail probability 0.00035714 per primary bound. It needs at least 155 independent genuine test units per cohort to certify 5% even at zero false alerts. With zero observed discordances, the two-point native noninferiority comparison needs 428 independent fake cases. The mathematical minimum for a five-point improvement is 15 if every case improves, an unrealistic best case. The exact conditional-sign interval uses finite-sample coverage and avoids separately paying for noise in two nearly equal discordance probabilities. Its power remains limited when the true native difference is near zero.

For small identity-linked cohorts, plan for an inconclusive result on the tight native margin even when every recording is scored successfully. To seek high-power confirmation, enroll a sufficiently large, authorized, independent population and prospectively amend its role and the cohort list before the campaign is sealed. Preserve the smaller cohort as a transport evaluation with its own uncertainty. If acquisition cannot support that scope, report the prespecified measurements and limits; do not add external cases conditionally on a disappointing observed result.


All registered cohorts remain in the analysis. By default they are all confirmation cohorts. To add prospectively enrolled populations and retain a smaller cohort for transport, register, for example, `analysis.cohorts: [dfdc, new_cohort, kodf]` and `analysis.confirmation_cohorts: [dfdc, new_cohort]`. Two independent confirmation cohorts remain necessary in confirmatory mode. Both selection and training claims, replication and seed checks use that frozen subset. Multiplicity conservatively includes all cohorts, and the role appears with every cohort-specific output. Inspect `capture_sessions.csv` for session-by-session performance. The exact confidence statements condition on the registered receiver processes/settings. Random future sessions and networks require independent sampling at that level; shared conditions or common disruptions cannot be counted as independent sessions.
